# Quick guide for inspection teams

## Before the visit
- Sign in with the login ID and temporary password sent by the Directorate. Set your own password when asked.
- Open **My inspections**. Then open your assigned ITI **once while you have internet**. The form will then reopen on your phone even if the network drops at the ITI.
- On Android Chrome, you can choose **Add to Home screen** for one-tap access.

## During the visit
- **Moving around the form:** use the part menu at the top (on a phone) or on the left (on a computer). **Save and continue** moves to the next part.
- **Saving:** entries save by themselves within a couple of seconds. The status line shows **All changes saved**.
- **Without network:** the status shows **Offline — changes kept on this device**. Keep working, and the entries are sent automatically when the network returns. Do not clear the browser data before then.
- **Answering:**
  - Tap **C / P / N / NA** to answer a compliance item. Tap the selected answer again to clear it.
  - A green dot next to the S.No means the item is answered.
- **Tables** (B1, D1, E2, F1, G1, J3 and others): use **+ Add row** for each trade or unit.
- **Evidence:** **Take photo** opens the camera, and **Attach file** picks a photo or PDF. Photos need a connection to upload. If you are offline, take them with the phone camera and attach them later.
- **A8 location:** use **Use current location** to fill in the geo-coordinates.

## Finishing
- **Declaration:** each member ticks their own declaration in **J8**. This is recorded with the date and time.
- **Signed acknowledgement:** photograph the Principal's signed acknowledgement and attach it under J8.
- **Final submission (team leader):** open **Review & submit**. Check the completeness table and any items listed, tick the confirmation and press **Final submission**. The form then locks.
- **If the Directorate returns the form:** the reason is shown at the top. Correct the entries and the leader submits again.
